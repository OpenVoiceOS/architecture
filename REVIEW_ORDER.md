## The two primitives

Everything in this wave is measured against them:

- **`session_id`** — per-conversation, per-peer state scoping on every Message.
- **the `source`/`destination` swap** — peer identity persists in the
  alternating reply pair.

Multi-tenant routing, satellite delivery, per-device personas and poll
addressing fall out of these two for free. Before any clause adds a mechanism,
the emergence trace runs: does the behaviour already fall out of `session_id`,
of the swap, or of their composition? Only a genuine failure on all three earns
an addition, and the PR body must say why emergence fails.

## Two design laws recorded this round

1. **`pipeline_id` ≡ `skill_id` namespace.** `session.pipeline` entries are
   matcher references, never distinct actors. A plugin instance has exactly one
   `pipeline_id`. (PIPELINE-1 §3, swept through STOP-1, COMMON-QUERY-1,
   PERSONA-1 and the appendix.)
2. **`reply` is a swap, not a rewrite.** `source` ← previous `destination`,
   `destination` ← previous `source`. `source` is not producer identity; the
   envelope carries none. (MSG-1 §5.2, matching `ovos-bus-client`
   `message.py:331`.)
